# Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone

A flip book of the storyboard. 135 pages and 762 panels. A full page holds 6 panels. A chapter adds a page when a page is full.

Chapters in the book:

1. The Boy Who Lived
2. Harry’s life with the Dursleys
3. The Keeper of the Keys
4. Diagon Alley
5. The Journey from Platform Nine and Three-Quarters
6. The Sorting Hat
7. First Lessons
8. The Potions Master
9. Flying class and the Seeker
10. The Midnight Duel
11. Hallowe’en
12. Quidditch
13. Nicolas Flamel
14. The Mirror of Erised
15. Norbert the Norwegian Ridgeback
16. The Forbidden Forest
17. They decide to protect the Stone
18. Through the Trapdoor
19. The Man with Two Faces
20. The hospital wing and the explanation
21. The House Cup

- [Flip book](index.html) — click the right page to turn forward and the left page to turn back. Each row is two panels: half and half, or a 2:1 or 1:2 split. The long frame is kept for wide views of the street, the sky, and the house.
- Storyboard: [Chapter 1 in the panel layout](../reference/harry-potter-philosophers-stone-panels.md).
- Drawing rules: [rules.md](../reference/rules.md), with wardrobe, character, and prop locks beside it. Draw a new panel at the printed size in that file. An old jpg may be larger, so do not copy its pixel size.

Character reference sheets live in `art/characters/` so the same people keep the same faces and clothes from panel to panel. The written locks in `reference/` win when a sheet is vague.

Once this folder is on the `main` branch, GitHub Pages serves the flip book at `/flipbook/`.
