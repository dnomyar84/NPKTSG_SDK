# Prop locks

One description each. If an object appears twice, it is the same object. Size, color, and glow do not get a new design because the panel is a close-up.

## Philosopher’s Stone

A small irregular stone the color of blood, about the size of a large walnut, warm, with a dull inner glow. The surface is rough. It is not a cut ruby, not a smooth pebble, not a clear crystal, and not jewelry. It fits in a child’s palm and makes a hard warm lump in a robe pocket. The same stone in the book engraving, in the Mirror, in Harry’s pocket, and in the hand Quirrell sees but cannot take.

In Gringotts it is hidden: one small parcel in dirty brown paper and string, on a stone table. The stone itself is not visible there. Do not unwrap it early. Do not turn it into a necklace, a green gem, a large crystal, or a different red from panel to panel.

Dumbledore says it will be destroyed. Do not show a second stone.

## Wands

A wand spell needs a thin line from that wand’s tip to the target. See [rules.md](rules.md).

- **Harry.** Holly and phoenix feather. Slender, warm brown, a slightly thicker handle, about as long as his forearm. The same wand from Ollivander’s shop onward. It feels comfortable in his hand. No tape, no jewels, no new carving later.
- **Ron.** Charlie’s old wand. Darker, worn, bent or chipped, spellotape around the middle. It looks unreliable. It is the wand that lifts the club. Do not give him a sleek new wand this year.
- **Hermione.** A slim smooth medium-brown wand, no tape, no bend, held properly. The exact film carving is easy to over-specify. Do not add gems or a unique sculpture. Keep this one wand.
- **Hagrid.** The pink umbrella is the wand. Slightly worn, a curved handle, the same pink every time. It lives in the moleskin coat or in his fist. The spark, the brick taps, and any spell leave from the umbrella tip. He does not also carry a separate wand.
- **Other witches and wizards.** One wand each, the same stick every time. Dumbledore’s is long and pale. Snape’s is thin and dark. McGonagall’s is straight and dark. Do not add ornaments you are not sure of.

The silver device Dumbledore uses on street lamps is a lighter shape, not his wand. The wand lights a lamp. The device puts lamps out.

## Remembrall

Neville’s. A small clear glass sphere, white smoke inside, pocket-sized. It turns red when he has forgotten something. The same ball in his pocket, at Draco’s fingers, and in Harry’s fist after the dive. It is not a marble and not a crystal ball on a stand.

## Sorting Hat

One very old brown leather hat, patched, a wide slumped brim, a frayed point, a rip near the brim that is the mouth. It sits on a plain three-legged wooden stool. The same hat sings, sits on Hermione, Draco, Neville, Ron, and Harry, and does not become a new hat per child.

## Invisibility cloak

Silvery gray cloth, fluid like water, folds small, shimmering. Worn, the body vanishes. Shoes, a tail, or a hem can stick out when the cloak is too small for the crate. The same cloth in the parcel, on Harry at Christmas, over the crate, and over Filch’s arm after Harry drops it. It is not a gray bedsheet.

## Golden Snitch

A gold ball about the size of a walnut, with tiny silver wings. The same Snitch Harry catches in his mouth. It is not the Quaffle and not a Bludger.

The Quaffle is a larger red leather ball, no wings. A Bludger is a black iron ball, smaller than the Quaffle, violent. Three different balls. Do not swap them.

## Harry’s broom

The match broom is a clean fast Nimbus Two Thousand, polished wood. School brooms in flying class are older and plainer. Do not draw the Nimbus in the first flying lesson, and do not draw a school broom at the match.

## Letters

Thick cream parchment, green ink, the Hogwarts crest (lion, eagle, badger, and snake around an H), a red wax seal. The same envelope whether it lands in the eggs, sticks to a window, or fills the fireplace. Do not invent a modern printed letter. The address can show as green handwriting. Do not paste a full letter.

## Hogwarts Express

A scarlet steam locomotive with black bands and gold lining, the words Hogwarts Express on the tender, steam, old corridor carriages with compartments. The same train at the start of term and at the end. Platform 9¾ is the brick barrier between platforms 9 and 10. The barrier is brick, not a gate with a sign that says the fraction until they are through.

## Hourglasses

Four giant hourglasses at the side of the Great Hall, tall as a student. Gryffindor rubies, Hufflepuff yellow stones, Ravenclaw blue stones, Slytherin emeralds. The book calls Hufflepuff’s stones diamonds. Draw them yellow so the house reads. At the leaving feast, green starts far ahead of red. Jewels pour when points are given. Do not invent a fifth glass, and do not use silver for Ravenclaw.

## Norbert’s egg

One egg. Larger than a pumpkin, black, leathery, scaled, sitting in the coals of Hagrid’s fire, rocking when it is ready. The crack is a bright line in that black shell. It is not a chicken egg and not a second color.

## Mirror of Erised

One mirror. As tall as a classroom ceiling, an ornate gold carved frame, two clawed feet, the inscription along the top in mirror writing: Erised stra ehru oyt ube cafru oyt on wohsi. It shows desire, not the room behind the viewer. The same mirror under the dusty sheet at Christmas and uncovered in the last chamber. Do not give it a new frame underground. Harry’s parents in the glass are the James and Lily in [characters.md](characters.md).

## Flute

A small wooden flute Harry plays at Fluffy. Notes travel from it to the heads. See [flute-and-fluffy.md](flute-and-fluffy.md). The same little flute in his belt afterward. It is not a wand.

## Chocolate Frogs

Bright wizard sweet boxes, a chocolate frog, and a card. The card that recurs is Dumbledore: long silver beard, half-moon spectacles, purple. It is not Nicolas Flamel’s portrait. Flamel stays the book engraving.

## House Cup

One large gleaming cup by the staff table at the leaving feast. Green and silver drapes behind it while Slytherin thinks it is theirs. It does not change shape when the points change. The hourglasses, not a new cup, show the score.
