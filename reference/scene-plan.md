# Scene plan

A scene is one place and one stretch of time, and it takes one page or more. A chapter has as many scenes as the story needs. Same background, weather, light, and wardrobe on every panel of a scene. Page 1 of a new scene walks the reader in before the talk: from outside to inside, or from the previous place to this one, then one panel of what the people were already doing. A time jump or a costume change needs a caption or an action still, and usually a new scene. A funny beat that does not fit stays in its scene on an added page. It does not become a new chapter.

Novel chapter titles stay as they were set. Extra scenes use our own names. Nicolas Flamel stays before the Mirror of Erised.

This is the drawing plan. It does not paste the novel. Picture files stay named as they were drawn (`c7-p2-p1.jpg` is a file, not a promise about the new page number). **EXISTING** means that file still belongs to that moment. **TO DRAW** means no file yet. Do not point an old jpg at a new moment.

The book before this plan was 20 chapters and 80 pages, four pages a chapter. This plan is **20 chapters and 136 pages**. A later split that pulled the classrooms into a separate First Lessons chapter is not this plan. Those rooms stay inside The Potions Master, one scene each.

`flipbook/story.js` already has the pages for the worst mixes, the broom in the face, and the troll’s nostril. That file is **20 chapters and 101 pages**. Every old picture except the Flamel montage is still attached to the same words. The montage file `art/c12-p1-p5.jpg` is retired. It shows three places in one frame. Do not reuse it for a single room. The other inserts below are not in `story.js` yet. Put them between the files named here. Do not renumber the old files.
